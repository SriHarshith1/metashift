##

> We're soon coming up on the Worlds patch (which is the patch *after* this one), so our first round of pro-focused Summoner's Rift changes are coming up here. We've identified some potential fringe picks for the tournament that could also provide some normal play variety without becoming disruptive with a nudge up in power, and knocking some individual meta outliers as well (and also Nasus). Systemically, the top lane role quest has been quite successful at freeing up top laners to skip Teleport, so we think increasing their map influence through that spell (both if you take it from the start of game or just through the quest) is a fair place to give the role some power.

## Champions

### Aatrox

> We think The Darkin Blade has lost a fair bit of power with Sundered Sky nerfs that were aimed more at opening up the jungle meta, so we are giving him a bump so he remains a world-ending threat.

---

#### W - Infernal Chains

* **Cooldown**: 20 / 18 / 16 / 14 / 12 seconds ⇒ **18 / 16.5 / 15 / 13.5 / 12 seconds**

---

#### E - Umbral Dash

* **Health Heal Ratio**: 1.1% per 100 bonus health ⇒ **1.3% per 100 bonus health**

### Aphelios

> Aphelios has been kiting the spotlight for a while now, even though he's a hypercarry in an enchanter meta that should favor him, so we're looking to give him a little boost to encourage him to get out of his comfort zone.

---

#### Calibrum

* **Moonshot Damage**: 70 - 160 (+42 - 60% bonus AD) (+100% AP) ⇒ **80 - 170 (+42 - 60% bonus AD) (+100% AP)**

---

#### Severum

* **Healing**: 2 - 7.1%, increased to 5 - 17.75% ⇒ **2 - 8%, increased to 5 - 20%**

---

#### Gravitum

* **Slow**: 30% for 2.5 seconds, decaying to 10% after 0.7 seconds ⇒ **30% for 2.5 seconds, decaying to 15% after 0.7 seconds**

---

#### Infernum

* **Duskwave Cooldown**: 9 / 8.25 / 7.5 / 6.75 / 6 seconds ⇒ **8 / 7.25 / 6.5 / 5.75 / 5 seconds**

---

#### Crescendum

* **Mini-Crescendum Damage**: 15% AD, decreasing by 1.5% AD to a minimum of 5% AD for the 8th+ chakram ⇒ **16% AD, decreasing by 1.5% AD to a minimum of 5% AD for the 9th+ chakram**

### Lucian

> Years ago, we added the Vigilance Passive so Lucian bot players didn't feel like their champ was being nerfed because of mid laners, which ended up being *super effective*. We think now is a good opportunity to adjust the tuning of the passive and strike a balance between both roles.

---

---

#### Q - Piercing Light

* **Base Damage**: 80 / 115 / 150 / 185 / 220 (+100% bonus AD) ⇒ **90 / 130 / 170 / 210 / 250 (+100% bonus AD)**

### Master Yi

> Somewhat overdue, but because Duskblade was removed a while ago, we're removing the Ability Haste penalty we had to apply to his Q CDR mechanic. This should have minimal impact on Summoner's Rift, but we see you Mayhem and Arena!

#### Q - Alpha Strike

### Nasus

> Nasus is difficult to keep down these days, and part of that is how quickly getting his early stacks leads to more reliable stacking. These higher highs will be compensated with some lower lows through some more vulnerability early on with lower damage and lifesteal lane sustain values off of that, and a bit longer before he really comes online.

---

#### Q - Siphoning Strike

* **Bonus Damage**: 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 / 120 ⇒ **30 / 50 / 70 / 90 / 110**

### Nocturne

> Nocturne has been a popular and powerful jungler in both pro and solo-queue for a while, so we're hitting the map presence and dueling power he benefits from through his ultimate.

#### R - Paranoia

### Poppy

### Rumble

## Items

#### World Atlas and Runic Compass

> We think melee supports have too much of their power in roaming and all-ins and are too weak in sustained laning. In response, we're shifting the flat health to health regen on World Atlas and Runic Compass, which will benefit them more due to their higher base health regen values.

---

* **Health**: 30 / 100 / 200 ⇒ **0 / 60 / 200**
* **Health Regeneration**: 25% / 50% / 75% ⇒ **50% / 75% / 75%**

## Systems

#### Teleport Cooldown

> As the meta has continued to adjust to role quests, it's become clear that top lane is slightly weak, especially for split pushing. We're in turn buffing the cooldown of both the free role quest Teleport and upgraded Unleashed Teleport to promote those alternative strategies.

---

* **Top Role Quest's Free Teleport Cooldown**: 420 seconds ⇒ **390 seconds**
* **Top Role Quest's Upgraded Unleashed Teleport Cooldown**: 330 - 240 seconds ⇒ **300 - 210 seconds**
* **Non-Top Unleashed Teleport Cooldown**: 330 - 240 seconds ⇒ **Unchanged**
