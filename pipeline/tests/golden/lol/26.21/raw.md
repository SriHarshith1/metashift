## Mid-Patch Updates

### October 22, 2026 Updates

#### Varus

> This patch we did work to adjust Varus' top and bottom power skew, which involved shifting the balance between his Q and W for laning. While the resulting changes have seemed promising, we unfortunately added a major bug to his Q during this work, causing it to no longer deal reduced damage to subsequent targets. We are fixing that bug, and compensating with a buff.

* Passive Bonus AD and AP on Kill/Takedown: (1.1 per 10% AS) as bonus AD and AP ⇒ 1.4 per 10% AS
* Q Minimum Damage: 50 - 230 ⇒ 50 - 250

## Champions

### Azir

> We've kept the Emperor of the Sands’ mana pool pretty dry to make sure he needs to use soldier attacks properly for lane control early instead of just spamming [Q] Conquering Sands, but this is overly restrictive now that there are strong rank up incentives on his [W] Arise! We’re buffing his Mana Growth so he can more freely use his abilities to contest lane.

### Caitlyn

> Caitlyn’s been at a very high pick and ban rate for most players for months now despite adjustments to other marksmen and the bot meta, so it's time to nerf her. We have felt that her late-game scaling has been too strong for a while now, so we’re moving some of her power from her eyepopping full build headshots into her early game Q damage to rebalance her power curve.

### Taric

> We're smoothing out some of the rough edges in Taric’s kit to allow him to shine a bit more. Way back in the day, Taric’s W was adjusted to combat the popular Master Yi + Taric funnel strats. A consequence of this was that his spells would fizzle if they were cast at the same time that his bound ally became untargetable. We believe this harsh restriction to how his abilities function is no longer necessary given the existence of Role Quests, so we're partially reverting this change so that spells now work on untargetable allies, though we are still keeping an existing exemption on [E] Dazzle for champions that disappear on screen because the gameplay is too unclear (sorry, Master Yi).

---

#### Q - Starlight’s Touch

* **Cast Behavior**: Q casts when allies are untargetable will now successfully fire.

---

#### E - Dazzle

* **Cast Behavior**: E casts when allies are untargetable will now successfully fire.
* **Tooltip**: Updated to include “Dazzle will not cast from Vanished allies”.

---

#### R - Cosmic Radiance

* **Cast Behavior**: R casts when allies are untargetable will now successfully fire.

## Items

### Bastion Breaker

> Bastion Breaker was buffed into being a bit of a generic stat stick in order to give it a proper swing in the meta. Now, we’re nerfing that generic power with a QoL improvement to keep it a satisfying item in its niche.

* **Lethality**: 22 ⇒ **18**
* **Shaped Charge Damage**: 50 Melee + 1.5/Lethality / 25 Ranged + 0.75/Lethality ⇒ **40 Melee + 1.5/Lethality / 20 Ranged + 0.75/Lethality**
* **Sabotage Duration**: 90s ⇒ **Infinite**
* **Sabotage Duration Context**: This is how long you have to use the bonus damage against objectives.

---

### Dawncore

> Dawncore is the enchanter capstone that isn’t, and currently only Soraka picks it up. Even when other supports get to full build, they often prefer other purchases. We’re buffing its AP value to make it less purely bound to Healing and Shielding.

* **Ability Power per 100% Base Mana Regeneration**: 10 ⇒ **15**

---

### Gluttonous Greaves

> The greaves are overperforming for marksmen, even when getting very few stacks, so we’re hitting it with a nerf to its cost to delay early acquisitions from getting too much value.

* **Total Cost**: 1000g ⇒ **1100g**

---

### Hubris

> While Hubris has been a bit weak for melee champions, it’s clearly been overperforming for ranged users. We’re delineating between the two now to encourage melees and disincentivize ranged users from building it.

* **Base Bonus Attack Damage**: 12 ⇒ **15 Melee / 10 Ranged**
* **Bonus Attack Damage per Stack**: 3 ⇒ **3 Melee / 2 Ranged**
