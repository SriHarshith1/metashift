> In 26.17 we’ve got a small amount of relief for top lane with Nasus and Vayne nerfs, and some followup on last patch to a handful of champions and items. We’re buffing a variety of melee AD carries, like Irelia, Qiyana, Yasuo, and Yone, who’ve been forced to snowball-or-lose for a while instead of having a reliable endgame to scale towards. Xerath continues to overperform in the mid lane in particular, as do Nocturne and Graves in the jungle. Finally, Sundered Sky wasn’t hit quite as hard as we were looking for, and we’re also looking to give Stormrazor a place in some builds again.

## Champions

### Aurelion Sol

> Aurelion Sol has come out the loser of last patch's mage adjustments, so we are giving him a buff to help him out. In particular, we are buffing his W cooldown so he can use it a bit more freely rather than only when a reset is likely. This should also make it a bit more clear to players that W is typically the stronger second skill to max, rather than E.
>
> ---
>
> #### Q - Breath of Light

* **Mana Cost Per Second**: 35 / 40 / 45 / 50 / 55 ⇒ **30 / 35 / 40 / 45 / 50**

---

#### W - Astral Flight

* **Cooldown**: 22 / 20.5 / 19 / 17.5 / 16s ⇒ **22 / 20 / 18 / 16 / 14s**

### Cho'Gath

> Cho's early game has lost pizzazz with Hail of Blades and Hextech Rocketbelt nerfs, so we are making it up with a bit of early damage when he does get those autos off.

---

#### E - Vorpal Spikes

* **Base Damage (x3 attacks)**: 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 (+30% AP) (+2.5% - 3.9% (+.5% Per Feast) Target Max HP) ⇒ **30 / 50 / 70 / 90 / 110 (+30% AP) (+2.5% - 3.9% (+.5% Per Feast) Target Max HP)**

### Graves

> Graves has had a number of indirect buffs over the last few patches and this has put him in a pretty strong spot. We are looking to soften a bit of his ability to snowball the game with high AD builds.
>
> ---
>
> #### Q - End of the Line

* **Initial Damage**: 50 / 75 / 100 / 125 / 150 (+65% bonus AD) ⇒ **50 / 75 / 100 / 125 / 150 (+55% bonus AD)**
* **Return Damage**: 80 / 125 / 170 / 215 / 260 (+55 / 70 / 85 / 100 / 115% bonus AD) ⇒ **80 / 125 / 170 / 215 / 260 (+45 / 60 / 75 / 90 / 105% bonus AD)**

### Irelia

> Irelia is one of League’s highest stakes snowballing dependant skirmishers, but we feel like even when ahead she is failing to scale into a real carry threat

---

#### Q - Bladesurge

* **Damage**: 5 / 25 / 45 / 65 / 85 (+70% AD) ⇒ **5 / 25 / 45 / 65 / 85 (+80% AD)**

> Additionally, we are buffing some of her AP ratios to support wacky ARAM/Arena builds. We don’t expect this to make AP Irelia suddenly good for SR.

---

#### W - Defiant Dance

* **Damage Reduction Ability Power Ratio**: 7% per 100 AP (Halved for Magic Damage) (reduced to 75% value for Magic Damage in Arena) ⇒ **8% per 100 AP (Halved for Magic Damage) (reduced to 75% value for Magic Damage in Arena)**

---

#### R - Vanguard's Edge

* **Ability Power Ratio**: 70% ⇒ **100%** (It is already 100% in Arena, so this is standardizing the value)

### Nasus

> Nasus top has been able to benefit from the shift in his stacking paradigm. We are happy to see our favorite dog get his fill of treats, but want to make sure that we keep his power within reasonable bounds, therefore we are going to be tapping down some of Nasus' top specific strengths, like persistently lifestealing off of minions.

---

* **Life Steal**: 12 / 18 / 24% ⇒ **10 / 15 / 20%**

### Yasuo

> While Yasuo has definitely been able to snowball and carry games, we believe that similar to Irelia, he is too reliant on snowballing rather than just being a proper scaling carry. We also want to encourage him to prioritize early crit strike builds over Blade of the Ruined King, so we are reducing his crit damage penalty which will simultaneously encourage this as well help him scale without significantly impacting his early game snowball.

---

#### Passive - Way of the Wanderer

* **Critical Strike Damage Reduction**: -10% ⇒ **-5%**

### Yone
